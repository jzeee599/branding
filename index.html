Silicon Valley
Life Sciences Group

Based in Sunnyvale California, Silicon Valley Life Sciences Group (SVLSG) is a supplement manufacturer that brings biotechnology insight into everyday wellness.

We created a brand that reflects the company’s environment, combining the bright, “sunny” character of Sunnyvale with the research-driven mindset of Silicon Valley.

The identity emphasizes clarity, vitality, and scientific discipline, mirroring SVLSG’s commitment to producing evidence-based supplements that support long-term health.
